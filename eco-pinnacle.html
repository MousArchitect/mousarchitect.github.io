Eco Pinnacle

Eco Pinnacle is a high-rise design with a glass-fronted tower framed by brick-clad wings, set on a landscaped base. At street level, the plan pairs a tree-lined boulevard and a planted triangular plaza at the entrance with a terraced green amphitheater nearby, weaving planting and public space into the city block.
