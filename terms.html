Terms & Scope

These notes describe the general scope of MousArchitect services. A written proposal or agreement is required before paid work begins and controls if it differs from this summary.

Design services

MousArchitect provides landscape design and architectural design support. Services may include concept studies, plans, planting information, materials direction, 3D visuals, and review calls as described in the selected proposal.

Important exclusions

Services are not licensed architectural services. Stamped drawings, permit submissions, permit fees, construction management, surveying, and structural-engineering services are not included unless expressly agreed in writing. Required engineering or professional sign-off is handled separately by an appropriately licensed professional.

Pricing and revisions

Published prices are starting points. Final pricing depends on scope, site size, complexity, and deliverables. Revisions are limited to the number stated in the proposal; additional scope is quoted separately.

Client materials

The client is responsible for accurate site information, access to required measurements, and decisions or approvals needed to keep the project moving.
