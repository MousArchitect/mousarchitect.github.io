Frequently asked questions

What do I provide?

Photos, a short video, basic measurements, and any survey or site information you have. Aerial and county GIS data may supplement the information you provide.

Are permits or engineering included?

No. Permit submissions, permit fees, stamped documents, and structural-engineering services are separate scopes. Requirements are discussed before work begins.

What counts as a revision?

A revision is one consolidated round of requested design changes within the selected package. New scope or major redesigns are quoted separately.

How do payments work?

A written proposal confirms scope, timeline, and payment schedule before work begins. Package availability and larger or complex sites are confirmed after consultation.

Prices are starting points. Final scope depends on site size, complexity, information available, and the selected deliverables.
