Northport Thrive

Northport Thrive is an academic urban design project that reimagines a rural Washington town through ecology, mobility, and community identity. The master plan introduces connected public spaces, walkable paths, ecological infrastructure, and gathering areas that support long-term community life.

Project Video

Project Areas

Explore the three Northport Thrive areas and the design decisions that shape each public space.

Area 1 — Oasis Park

Oasis Park transforms underused land into a resilient community green space with recreation, shaded paths, and water-sensitive design.

Area 2 — Riversquare Park

Riversquare Park introduces a mixed-use town-center district that combines housing, retail, and public space to support walkability and economic vitality.

Area 3 — Civic Plaza

Civic Plaza acts as Northport’s cultural centerpiece, with flexible space for markets, festivals, daily gatherings, and a salmon-inspired public artwork.
