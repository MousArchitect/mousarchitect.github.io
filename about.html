About MousArchitect

I'm Mousa Hassan, the designer behind MousArchitect. I hold a B.S. in Landscape Architecture from Washington State University and a diploma in Architectural Technology from Jeddah College of Technology in Saudi Arabia. MousArchitect provides landscape and residential design services for homeowners across Washington.

My work ranges from residential yards and gardens to buildings and community spaces like Eco Pinnacle and Northport Thrive. My architecture background shapes how I see a site: I think about how buildings and outdoor spaces work together, so the landscape feels like part of the design, not an afterthought.

I start every project by listening to what my clients want for their space, their goals, and their budget. From there, I develop concept sketches, 3D renderings, and a clear plan ready for construction, so my clients can see the finished project before work begins.

Whether it's a backyard retreat, a front-yard makeover, an ADU concept, or a bigger idea, I'd love to hear about your project.

What I bring

  • Landscape and architectural design thinking in one workflow
  • Washington-aware, water-wise and native-plant strategies
  • Clear plans and 3D visuals that make decisions easier

Portfolio note: Current work includes academic and concept projects. Residential client work will be added as it becomes available.
