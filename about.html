About MousArchitect

I'm Mousa Hassan, a designer working where landscape and architecture meet. I hold a diploma in architecture technology and a degree from Washington State University, and MousArchitect is my studio for both.

My work ranges from residential yards and gardens to buildings and community spaces like Northport Thrive and Eco Pinnacle. My architecture background shapes how I see a site: I think about how buildings and outdoor spaces work together, so the landscape feels like part of the design, not an afterthought.

I start every project by listening to what my clients want for their space, their goals, and their budget. From there, I develop concept sketches, 3D renderings, and a clear plan ready for construction, so my clients can see the finished project before work begins.

Whether it's a backyard retreat, a front-yard makeover, or a bigger idea, I'd love to hear about your project.
