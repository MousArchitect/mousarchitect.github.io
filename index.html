About me

I'm Mousa Hassan, founder of MousArchitect. I graduated from Washington State University, and I also hold a diploma in Architectural Technology from Jeddah College of Technology in Saudi Arabia. My work focuses on creating spaces that bring people together and respect the natural environment, from residential landscapes to sustainable architecture that blends seamlessly with its site.

I work in AutoCAD, Rhino, SketchUp, Revit, Lumion, and Adobe Creative Suite, and I enjoy turning ideas into functional, beautiful places.
